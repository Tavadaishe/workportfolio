Planning & Settlement Analysis
QGIS · OpenStreetMap · Digitizing

Building Footprint Mapping — Chegutu Built-Up Area

Digitized and mapped building footprints across Chegutu to build a baseline settlement inventory, revealing a dense core along the highway/rail corridor and a disconnected low-density cluster to the northwest.

QGISOpenStreetMapCartography
Case details
Problem

Chegutu Municipality lacked a consolidated, current inventory of built structures to inform service extension and zoning review.

Approach

Digitized building footprints against high-resolution basemap imagery, cross-referenced with OSM road and hydrology layers, and produced a cartographic output for planning staff.

Outcome

Identified the highway/rail corridor as the primary growth spine and flagged an unplanned peripheral cluster for ground-truthing before any zoning decision.

Land Use & Zoning
QGIS · Cadastral Data · Spatial Database

High Density Property Land Use Classification

Classified thousands of individually numbered high-density stands into Commercial, Institutional, Residential, Open Space and Light Industry categories, with buffer zones flagged for review.

QGISCadastral SurveyZoning
Case details
Problem

Property valuation and change-of-use processing needed an accurate, stand-level land use layer aligned to municipal records.

Approach

Cross-matched thousands of cadastral stand numbers against ground-truthed land use categories, applying a consistent classification and buffer scheme across wards.

Outcome

Delivered a ward-wide classified layer used directly by the Spatial Planning Department for valuation and subdivision decisions.

WASH Infrastructure
QGIS · Spatial Database · Analytics

Portable Water Distribution Network & Analytics

Mapped Chegutu's full reticulation network from the raw waterline intake through distribution mains (63mm–375mm), and built a supporting analytics dashboard of pipe diameter and material composition.

QGISWASH PlanningData Visualization
Case details
Problem

Council needed a spatial inventory of the water network and visibility into asset condition risk for infrastructure planning.

Approach

Mapped raw and portable waterlines with pipe diameters, then quantified pipe size distribution and material composition (97.3% AC, 2.6% PVC, 0.1% GL) in a companion chart set.

Outcome

Surfaced the dominance of aging asbestos-cement (AC) pipe as a key asset-renewal risk for council WASH planning.

Google Earth Engine · Landsat · JavaScript

Environmental Monitoring — NDVI & Land Surface Temperature, Sanyati

Computed vegetation health (NDVI) and land surface temperature across the Sanyati catchment from Landsat imagery in Google Earth Engine to flag degradation and thermal stress zones.

Google Earth EngineRemote SensingGeo AI
Case details
Problem

Environmental degradation and land surface heating needed to be tracked across a large catchment without ground survey.

Approach

Scripted an NDVI and emissivity-corrected LST workflow in Google Earth Engine over Landsat scenes, deriving values from -0.50 (bare/degraded) to healthy vegetation, and 18.5°C–42.9°C surface temperature.

Outcome

Produced a repeatable, cloud-based monitoring script for ongoing degradation and thermal-stress tracking, extensible to burn severity mapping.

Chegutu Land Bank Map

Mapped and classified all council land bank holdings — state land, solar farm sites, industrial extension, open spaces/wetlands and residential land banks — to support land allocation decisions.

QGISLand AdministrationCouncil Planning
Case details
Problem

Council needed a single reference map of all land bank parcels and their designated use to guide investment and residential allocation.

Approach

Compiled and classified holdings including Umvovo State land (813 ha), a 67.5 ha solar farm site, a 59.9 ha industrial extension inclusive of a sewer treatment plant, and residential land banks totalling over 590 ha.

Outcome

Gave council a defensible spatial basis for prioritizing residential, industrial and renewable-energy land release.

Cadastral Parcel Mapping — Industrial & High Density Stands

Digitized and verified stand-level parcel boundaries and stand numbers for industrial and high-density stands, integrated into the municipal spatial database for subdivision and ownership records.

QGISCadastral SurveyDatabase Management
Case details
Problem

Subdivision and change-of-use applications required accurate, individually numbered parcel boundaries linked to council records.

Approach

Digitized parcel geometry from survey diagrams, matched stand numbers to the municipal database, and validated boundaries against adjacent land parcels.

Outcome

Produced a verified cadastral layer supporting faster subdivision approvals and reduced boundary disputes.
